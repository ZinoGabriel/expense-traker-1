⚡

Manual SMS Parser

Copy and paste a bank alert into the SMS Parser. The app extracts the amount and helps you add it as an expense. It cannot read your phone's SMS inbox.

📂

CSV Import and Backup

Import common bank CSV formats, including quoted descriptions with commas. Review the import summary, skip duplicates, and export your expense history as a backup.

💱

Location-Based Currency

Choose a supported country at sign-up to set your display currency. Convert amounts between supported currencies using built-in estimated rates.

🎯

Budgets That Travel

Set overall and category spending limits. Your budgets and expense history sync to your account so they’re available when you sign in on another device.

📊

Detailed Spending Insights

Filter expenses by date and inspect each transaction. Compare daily, weekly, or monthly totals, or view spending by category or item.

💬

Spendy Assistant with Gemini AI

Use local summaries and expense logging, or request optional Gemini AI advice from your monthly totals and category aggregates. Voice depends on browser support.

☁️

Supabase Cloud Database

Supabase Auth and a protected Supabase database store each account's expenses and budget settings, so they can sync across devices. The app owner must configure Supabase before cloud sync works.

🔒

Private Account Data

Passwords are handled by Supabase Auth, and expense rows are protected by per-user database policies. Use a trusted deployment and do not enter bank login details.

📱

Installable Web App

Install the Progressive Web App from a supported browser using its Add to Home Screen option.
