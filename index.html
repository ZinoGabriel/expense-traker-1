⚡

Manual SMS Parser

Copy and paste a bank alert into the SMS Parser. The app extracts the amount and helps you add it as an expense. It cannot read your phone's SMS inbox.

📂

CSV Statement Import

Import a CSV bank statement to add multiple expenses at once. Review the imported entries in your expense history.

💱

Currency Converter

Shopping on global stores online? Input spending in USD ($), GBP (£), or EUR (€) and convert them automatically into local Nigerian Naira values.

🎯

Budget Limits

Set an overall budget and category limits, then review your weekly, monthly, and yearly spending totals.

📊

Charts and History

View your saved expenses in a history table and see spending grouped by category in a chart.

☁️

Account Sync Across Devices

Sign in with the same account on another device to access your expense history. This requires the app owner to configure Supabase cloud storage.

🔒

Private Account Data

Passwords are handled by Supabase Auth, and expense rows are protected by per-user database policies. Use a trusted deployment and do not enter bank login details.

📱

Installable Web App

Install the Progressive Web App from a supported browser using its Add to Home Screen option.
